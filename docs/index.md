# Adaptive Intelligence Circle

**Independent ethical coordination infrastructure — from Global South outward.**

AIC is a non-profit, open-source-oriented organization and protocol family founded in April 2025.  

We build systems where **refusal, audit, and human meaning** sit near authorization — not only as policy text after launch.

> This is not a consumer product. It is infrastructure-shaped work: kernel constraints, governance procedure, and long-horizon meaning preservation.

---

## What AIC is

- An **independent** project for an ethical coordination layer (human–AI systems)
- **Ethical Kernel** + governance evaluate (Allow / Deny / NeedHuman)
- **Third Path**: refuses pure state capture *and* pure commercial rule-power over the protocol
- Long-range aim: **World Meaning Infrastructure (WMI)** — substrate, not a single app
- **Human Meaning Network (HMN)**: meaning-critical acts stay human-gated

## What AIC is not

- A United Nations body, program, or endorsement  
- A Big Tech ethics skin or subsidiary narrative  
- A token-voting scheme that sells protocol law  
- A replacement for national law  
- A finished global network or a guarantee of civilizational outcomes  

**Partner ≠ owner ≠ regulator ≠ shield.** Legal entity or fiscal host does not erase applicable law or the Charter.

---

## Why this exists

Much of the industry scales compute and surface-level alignment under commercial and geopolitical pressure.  
Fewer projects treat **intellectual infrastructure** — who may act, what must be refused, what must stay human — as a first-class, inspectable system.

AIC works in that gap: slower to monetize, easier to misunderstand, necessary if coordination is not to default into capture or meaning collapse.

## Core principles (short)

1. **Transparency** — inspectable artifacts; no secret dual stack  
2. **Ethical-from-kernel** — fail-closed constraints near authorization  
3. **No token rule-power** — money does not buy protocol law  
4. **Human alignment** — augment and gate; do not hollow meaning  
5. **Long-term stability** — controlled procedure over uncontrolled scale
